# NekoNihongo Graded Japanese Curriculum 📚

## 1. Pedagogical Scope & Alignment

The NekoNihongo curriculum is designed around structured communicative competence and verified JLPT proficiency benchmarks.

- **Level A (Foundations)**:
  - Unit 1: Hiragana Foundations (Core vowels, K-row, S-row, T-row)
  - Unit 2: Hiragana Mastery & Everyday Greetings (Dakuten がざだば, Handakuten ぱ, Daily greetings)
  - Unit 3: Katakana Essentials & Global Loanwords (ア・イ・ウ・エ・オ, Food, Drinks, Tech, Countries)
- **Level B (Beginner Japanese / JLPT N5)**:
  - Unit 4: Self-Introductions & Topic Marker は (Identity, professions, nationality, demonstratives)
  - Unit 5: Actions & Objects: Particle を (Transitive verbs, food & beverage dining)
  - Unit 6: Movement & Destinations: に & へ (Going, coming, returning, clock times)
  - Unit 7: Action Locations & Means: Particle で (Places of events, tools, transport)
- **Level C (Elementary Japanese / JLPT N5-N4)**:
  - Unit 8: Describing Things: い & な Adjectives (Modifications, past forms, negatives)
  - Unit 9: Te-form & Polite Requests (Verb conjugation groups, 〜てください, 〜ています)
  - Unit 10: Existence, Reasons & Daily Life (あります / います, 〜から reason clauses)
